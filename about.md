# About this recipe box

This site is a transcription of three boxes of family recipe cards. Some are
handwritten, some are clipped from magazines and newspapers, and a few are
typed on index cards. They had never been in one place before.

## How it was made

The cards were scanned, then read with OCR and reconstructed into structured
recipes: an ingredient list with amounts, units and prep notes, and numbered
steps with timings. That structured form is what you see on each recipe page,
and it is what makes the search and filters on the
[recipes page]({{ '/recipes/' | relative_url }}) possible.

The source of truth is an Obsidian vault. A script reads the vault and
generates this site, so corrections are made to the notes and the site is
rebuilt &mdash; the HTML is never edited by hand.

## A word of caution

These are old cards, read by a machine, so transcription errors are possible.
Where a card was vague, a typical time was filled in rather than invented
detail. Read the whole recipe before you start.

The cards are not dated. Nothing on this site claims when a recipe is from,
because in most cases there is no way to know.

Nutrition figures are estimated from USDA data against the parsed ingredient
list. They are a rough guide for comparing recipes, not a measurement.

## Duplicates

The same recipe sometimes appears on more than one card, in slightly different
handwriting or with slightly different amounts. Where two cards look like the
same dish, each recipe page links to the other under *Possibly the same
recipe* rather than picking a winner.
