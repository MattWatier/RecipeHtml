# Oven-Baked Broccoli “Au Gratin”

## Ingredients
- 1 head broccoli
- Salted water
- 1 garlic clove, chopped fine
- 2 T. Pecorino cheese
- Pepper
- 3 T. breadcrumbs

## Directions
1. Preheat the oven to **425°**.
2. Bring a large saucepan of **salted water** to a boil.
3. Cook the **1 head broccoli**: stems for **12–15 minutes**. Remove. Cook the florets **2–3 minutes**. Drain.
4. Oil a medium baking casserole. Arrange the broccoli in it.
5. Sprinkle with the **1 garlic clove, chopped fine** and **2 T. Pecorino cheese**. Season with **pepper**. Sprinkle with **3 T. breadcrumbs**.
6. Bake until the crumbs are browned, about **15 minutes** (typical).
