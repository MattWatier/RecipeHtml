# Moody's Four Berry Pie

## Ingredients
- 1 unbaked 9-inch pie shell, plus top crust
- 1 cup strawberries
- 1 cup blackberries
- 1 cup blueberries
- 1 cup raspberries
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 tablespoons tapioca
- Dash of salt
- ½ teaspoon cinnamon
- Butter, for dotting

## Directions
1. Put **1 cup strawberries**, **1 cup blackberries**, **1 cup blueberries**, and **1 cup raspberries** in an **unbaked 9-inch pie shell**.
2. Combine **1 cup sugar**, **2 tablespoons tapioca**, a **dash of salt**, and **½ teaspoon cinnamon**. Pour over the berries.
3. Dot with **butter**. Cover with the **top crust**.
4. Bake at **325°** for **1 hour**.
