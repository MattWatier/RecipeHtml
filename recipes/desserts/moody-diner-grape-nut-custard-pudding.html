# Moody Diner Grape Nut Custard Pudding

## Ingredients
- 4 eggs
- 1 c. sugar
- ¾ c. Grape-Nuts cereal
- ½ t. vanilla
- ¼ t. salt
- 1 qt. milk

## Directions
1. Beat the **4 eggs**.
2. Add the **1 c. sugar**, **¾ c. Grape-Nuts cereal**, **½ t. vanilla**, and **¼ t. salt**. Mix.
3. Stir in the **1 qt. milk**.
4. Pour into a **9×13** pan.
5. Bake **1 hour** at **350°**.

# Grape-Nut Pudding

(Stovetop version on the back of the card.)

## Ingredients
- 1 qt. milk
- 1 c. Grape-Nuts cereal
- ½ c. sugar
- 2 egg yolks
- 2½ T. flour
- 2 egg whites
- 2 T. sugar
- whipped cream, for serving

## Directions
1. In the top of a double boiler, combine and heat the **1 qt. milk** and **1 c. Grape-Nuts cereal**, about **5 minutes** (typical).
2. In a large bowl, mix the **½ c. sugar**, **2 egg yolks**, and **2½ T. flour**. Add a little of the milk to make a smooth paste.
3. Add to the warm milk. Cook until thickened, about **10 minutes** (typical).
4. Beat the **2 egg whites**. Add the **2 T. sugar**. Beat until stiff, about **3 minutes** (typical).
5. Fold the egg whites into the cooked pudding.
6. Serve with **whipped cream**.
